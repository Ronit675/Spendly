Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide you with a personal expense tracking experience, Spendly collects the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and password (encrypted) used for authentication.
  • Financial Data: Expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions that you manually log into the application.
  • Usage Data: Basic logs of how you interact with the application to help us improve the user experience.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To maintain your account and provide access to your personal expense records.
  • To generate spending reports, category breakdowns, and financial summaries.
  • To improve the functionality and performance of the Spendly application.
  • To communicate with you regarding account updates or service changes.

3. Data Storage & Security

We take the security of your financial data seriously. Your information is stored in a secure database with industry-standard protections. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal financial data to outside parties. We may use trusted third-party service providers (such as hosting platforms) to operate our application, but they are restricted from using your data for any purpose other than providing these services to us.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at: privacy@spendly.app

Spendly is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your financial data remains your own.
